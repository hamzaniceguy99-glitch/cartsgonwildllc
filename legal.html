What we provide

Carts Gon Wild provides supply, customization, street-legal conversion work and servicing of golf carts and low-speed vehicles by CARTS GON WILD LLC, a Florida limited liability company with document number L20000377034 on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations. TOP SPEED DETERMINES THE LEGAL CATEGORY OF THE MACHINE, and the category determines the obligations of its owner. A golf cart, being a vehicle designed and manufactured for operation on a golf course and not capable of exceeding 20 miles per hour, is exempt under Florida law from titling, registration and compulsory motor vehicle insurance, and its operation on public roads is restricted to roads and crossings designated for golf cart use by the authority having jurisdiction over that road. A LOW-SPEED VEHICLE, being a four-wheeled vehicle whose attainable top speed is greater than 20 but not greater than 25 miles per hour, IS A MOTOR VEHICLE: it must be titled and registered, it must be insured with personal injury protection and property damage liability coverage, its operator must hold a valid driver license, and it may be operated only on streets where the posted speed limit is 35 miles per hour or less. A low-speed vehicle must additionally comply with the federal motor vehicle safety standard applicable to low-speed vehicles at 49 CFR 571.500, which requires headlamps, front and rear turn signal lamps, taillamps, stop lamps, reflex reflectors, mirrors, a parking brake, a windshield of conforming glazing, a seat belt assembly at each designated seating position, and a vehicle identification number. A GOLF CART THAT IS MODIFIED SO THAT IT CAN EXCEED 20 MILES PER HOUR HAS CHANGED CATEGORY WHETHER OR NOT ITS OWNER INTENDED IT TO, and modifications affecting attainable top speed include motor and controller changes, gearing changes and increases in rolling diameter arising from larger tires. ABOVE 25 MILES PER HOUR NO ROAD CATEGORY APPLIES: such a machine is neither a golf cart nor a low-speed vehicle, cannot meet the federal safety standards applicable to a passenger car, and CANNOT BE MADE STREET LEGAL. The company does not represent any machine capable of exceeding 25 miles per hour as street legal, lawful for road use or registrable, and declines work requested on that basis. STREET-LEGAL CONVERSION IS NOT ACHIEVED BY FITTING LAMPS: a machine presented for titling as a low-speed vehicle must satisfy the applicable federal standard and be capable of title in fact, and where that outcome cannot be achieved the company says so before work is undertaken rather than after. TITLING AND REGISTRATION ARE PERFORMED BY THE STATE, not by the company, and no particular outcome of any application to a state agency is promised. OPERATOR REQUIREMENTS: since 1 October 2023 a person under 18 years of age must hold a valid learner driver license or driver license to operate a golf cart on a public road, and a person 18 years of age or older must have in their possession a valid driver license or other valid government-issued photographic identification. Florida impaired driving law applies to the operation of a golf cart.
